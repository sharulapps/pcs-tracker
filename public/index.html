How the numbers get here

  1. Plan from ExcelPlanner uploads the monthly PRODUCTION PLAN SUMMARY. Each D / N cell becomes a plan for that date, shift, machine and model.
  2. Snap the PCSAt the end of the shift the supervisor photographs the PCS form.
  3. Gemini extractsThe Worker sends the photo to Gemini. Machine, model, shift and each hour's actual and reject come back as JSON.
  4. Human checkSupervisor corrects any misread numbers. The hourly plan is filled from the monthly plan's output/hour.
  5. FirestoreWorker saves plans to pcs_plans and PCS records to pcs_records.
  6. DashboardOutput, daily and hourly plan vs actual by machine, model and shift.
