How a PCS form reaches the dashboard

  1. SnapOperator or supervisor photographs the paper PCS form at the line.
  2. Cloudflare WorkerPage sends the photo to /api/extract. The Gemini key stays on the Worker.
  3. Gemini extractsHeader fields and each hour's plan, actual, reject and remarks come back as JSON.
  4. Human checkSupervisor corrects any misread numbers, then saves.
  5. FirestoreWorker writes the record to the pcs_records collection.
  6. DashboardOutput, daily and hourly plan vs actual update from the saved records.
